07 - SCORING LOGIC
===================

This document defines exactly how basketball events become points.
This is business logic built on top of AI detections — not a vision model.


V1 SCORING RULE (SIMPLE)
--------------------------

  1 point per made basket, regardless of shot position.
  Arc detection is deferred to v2.

This is displayed clearly in the UI so users understand the limitation.


THE EVENT CHAIN FOR AWARDING A POINT
--------------------------------------

For each point to be awarded, the system must observe this sequence:

  Step 1: Possession
    Player X (Team A or Team B) has the ball.
    Determined by: ball bounding box overlapping or nearest to player X
    for at least 10 consecutive frames.

  Step 2: Shot release
    The ball separates from all player bounding boxes and its velocity
    vector is directed toward the hoop.
    This is the "shot attempt" event. Timestamp is recorded here.

  Step 3: Made basket
    The ball enters the hoop region from above and exits below the rim.
    Alternatively: ball enters hoop region and possession changes to
    the other team without a visible rebound.

  Step 4: Attribution
    The point is credited to Team A or Team B based on the jersey color
    of the player who had possession at Step 2.

  Step 5: Write to database
    Insert one 'made_shot' row in shot_events.
    points_awarded = 1
    confidence = calculated score from Step 3 detection quality


CONFIDENCE SCORE CALCULATION
------------------------------

The confidence value on each shot event reflects how certain the system
is that the detection is correct. It is not a single model output — it
is a composite of several signals:

  Signal                              Weight
  ----------------------------------  ------
  Ball detected entering hoop region  High
  Ball velocity is downward at rim    High
  Ball exits below rim                High
  Possession change after event       Medium
  No rebound motion visible           Low

A simple weighted average of the signals that were observed produces
the final confidence. Thresholds:

  >= 0.75  →  high confidence, shown normally
  0.50-0.74 →  low confidence, flagged with a warning icon
  < 0.50   →  not counted as a basket


WHAT IS NOT IN V1 SCORING
---------------------------

  - 2-point shots (behind arc)     → deferred to v2
  - Free throws                    → not applicable in 3x3 (they score 1pt,
                                     same as inside arc shots; v2 if needed)
  - Offensive rebounds + putbacks  → counted as a new possession + shot
  - Turnovers                      → not tracked
  - Fouls                          → not tracked


V2 SCORING ADDITIONS (PLANNED)
--------------------------------

When arc detection is added in v2:

  FIBA 3x3 rules:
    Shooter's feet inside the arc → 1 point
    Shooter's feet behind the arc → 2 points

  Implementation requires:
    1. Detect the arc line on the court (Hough Circle or homography)
    2. Map the arc from video pixel coordinates to court coordinates
    3. At the moment of shot release, check whether the shooter's
       feet (bottom of bounding box) are inside or outside the arc
    4. Award 1 or 2 accordingly
