6. How basketball stats are actually produced

This is where your app becomes a sports app instead of just a computer vision app.

You need a stats engine.

The stats engine reads lower-level events and applies basketball rules.

Example event chain

Suppose the system detects:

player #30 controls ball
player #30 dribbles
player #30 shoots from beyond 3-point line
ball enters hoop
possession changes

Then stats engine says:

FGA +1
3PA +1
FGM +1
3PM +1
Points +3

That is not a vision model directly.
That is business logic built on AI detections.

Another example: rebound

System detects:

shot attempt by player #11
ball contacts rim/backboard and misses
player #30 gains possession immediately after miss

Stats engine says:

rebound for player #30
Another example: assist

This is harder.

System may detect:

player #30 passes to player #7
player #7 shoots quickly after catch
shot is made
no major possession break in between

Then stats engine may infer assist for player #30.

That is possible, but harder to get right than shot detection.

So for MVP, I would prioritize:

shot attempts
made shots
possessions
rebounds
basic passes
maybe assists later
