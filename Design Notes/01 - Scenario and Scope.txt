01 - SCENARIO AND SCOPE
=======================

THE EXACT SCENARIO WE ARE BUILDING FOR
---------------------------------------

- 3v3 half-court basketball game
- 6 players total (3 per team)
- Teams wear visually distinct jersey colors
- Video source: a single YouTube video, approximately 10 minutes long
- Fixed camera angle (not a broadcast — typically a phone or tripod recording)


WHY THIS SCENARIO IS FAVORABLE
--------------------------------

- Fewer players = fewer tracking collisions
- Different jersey colors = skip OCR entirely, color alone identifies teams
- Fixed file = not a live stream, we can process it in full before showing results
- Half-court = smaller play area, hoop stays in frame more consistently


WHAT THE SYSTEM OUTPUTS (v1)
------------------------------

For each made basket:
  - Which team scored (by jersey color)
  - Timestamp in the video
  - Confidence score (e.g. 0.81)

Aggregated per team:
  - Total points (flat 1 point per made basket for v1)

That is it. No assists, no rebounds, no individual player names.


THE SCORING CHAIN THE SYSTEM MUST FOLLOW
------------------------------------------

To award a point, the system must detect this sequence:

  1. A player has possession of the ball
  2. The ball leaves that player and travels toward the hoop
  3. The ball passes through the hoop (downward direction)
  4. The system attributes the basket to the team color of the shooting player

Everything else (dribbles, passes, possession chains) is intermediate
information used to support that conclusion, not an end output.


WHAT v1 DELIBERATELY SKIPS
-----------------------------

- Jersey number reading (OCR)
- Arc detection (no 1pt vs 2pt distinction — flat 1pt per basket)
- Individual player stats (only team totals)
- Rebounds, passes, assists, steals, blocks
- Per-frame data storage
- Live streaming support
- Multi-video analysis


HONEST ACCURACY EXPECTATION
-----------------------------

Approximately 70-85% accuracy on made basket detection.
This varies significantly by camera angle.

Best case: courtside angle where ball passing through hoop is clearly visible.
Worst case: far away or off-angle camera where the ball disappears near the rim.

The system will show a confidence score on every event.
Low-confidence detections are flagged for user review.
The system never claims 100% accuracy.


FUTURE DIRECTIONS (not scope for v1)
--------------------------------------

v2: Rebounds, passes, shot chart, highlight clips
v3: Assists, steals, team-level stats, multi-player tracking
Later: Brand/logo detection for marketing analytics (separate pipeline)
